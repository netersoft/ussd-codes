USSD Codes gathers the USSD codes of mobile operators in 8 African countries. No more searching for the code to check your balance, buy a data bundle, top up or send mobile money: find the code, tap it, done.

★ 8 COUNTRIES, 21 OPERATORS, MORE THAN 300 CODES
• Benin: MTN, Moov Africa, Celtiis
• Cameroon: MTN, Orange
• Côte d'Ivoire: MTN, Moov Africa, Orange
• Mali: Moov Africa, Orange
• Niger: Zamani Telecom, Moov Africa, Airtel
• Nigeria: MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile
• Senegal: Orange, Yas
• Togo: Moov Africa, Yas
The app opens straight on the operator of your SIM card.

★ ALL THE USEFUL CODES
• Balance and usage
• Data bundles and data balance
• Airtime top-up and transfers
• Mobile money: Orange Money, MTN MoMo, Moov Money, Flooz, Mixx by Yas, Celtiis Cash, Zamani Cash
• Customer service and operator services

★ CODES BUILT FOR YOU
For a code that needs a phone number, an amount or a PIN, just fill in the fields: the app builds the code as you type, with the right keyboard. Pick a number from your contacts. Your PIN is masked and never stored, and every code is confirmed before it runs.

★ ONE TAP
With the Phone permission, the app dials the code directly and shows the operator's reply. Otherwise, the code opens in your phone app, ready to call.

★ DATA BUNDLE COMPARATOR
Compare the data bundles of the operators by price, volume or price per GB (Benin, Togo and Orange Côte d'Ivoire for now).

★ FAVORITES AND PERSONAL CODES
• Star the codes you use most
• Add your own codes
• Quick Settings tile: open your favorite code from the notification panel
• Search across every country

★ YOUR PHONE'S CODES TOO
IMEI number, test menus and other codes of the phone itself, filtered by brand.

★ ALWAYS UP TO DATE
Operators change their codes: the catalog is updated without waiting for a new version of the app. The codes are on your phone, so the app works without an internet connection.
